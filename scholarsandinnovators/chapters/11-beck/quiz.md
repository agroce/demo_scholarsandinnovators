## Part A — Multiple choice

**1.** Why does Beck say he started collecting "complexity partitioning" strategies?

- (a) His professors required it
- (b) He was working on very large systems from the start
- (c) He didn't have the "galaxy brain" to write huge programs all at once, like his friend
- (d) Smalltalk forced him to

**2.** What does Beck call "the fallacy of the waterfall"?

- (a) That if we just think hard enough now, we won't have to change later
- (b) That testing should come last
- (c) That documentation is unnecessary
- (d) That customers know what they want

**3.** What is the one thing Beck says a programmer who wants to be great should understand?

- (a) Always use the newest language
- (b) Nothing beats repetition
- (c) Formal proofs are essential
- (d) Read the Agile Manifesto

**4.** What is the worst practice Beck identifies?

- (a) Writing too many tests
- (b) Pair programming with junior developers
- (c) Using LLMs
- (d) Over-indexing on features and under-indexing on futures

**5.** In Beck's account of Facebook's weekly incident reviews, who was walked out of the building?

- (a) People who blamed others instead of owning their part
- (b) Anyone who caused a costly outage
- (c) Anyone who caused a second incident
- (d) Managers whose teams had incidents

**6.** Why does Beck call an LLM coding assistant "the genie"?

- (a) It works by magic
- (b) It is always correct
- (c) It grants his wishes, but often not what he actually wanted
- (d) It lives in the cloud

**7.** Beck compares computer science education to:

- (a) law school
- (b) pre-med: necessary background, like organic chemistry, that you need only occasionally but critically
- (c) an apprenticeship
- (d) music conservatory
