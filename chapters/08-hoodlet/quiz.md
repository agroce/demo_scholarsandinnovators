## Part A — Multiple choice

**1.** According to Hoodlet, why do experts get more benefit from LLM coding tools than junior developers?

- (a) Experts have access to better models
- (b) Experts know when the output is wrong and when it is what they actually want
- (c) Junior developers aren't allowed to use them at work
- (d) Experts write longer prompts

**2.** Hoodlet contrasts software development's "builder's mentality" with security's:

- (a) auditor's checklist
- (b) manager's mindset
- (c) breaker's mindset
- (d) mathematician's mindset

**3.** What did Hoodlet tell Thermo Fisher teams was the key to patching security problems quickly?

- (a) Robust testing, so updates can ship without breaking things
- (b) Hiring more security staff
- (c) Rewriting everything in Rust
- (d) Air-gapping every device

**4.** Hoodlet's term "technical inflation" means:

- (a) codebases growing larger over time
- (b) the rising cost of developer salaries
- (c) shortcuts taken during development that must later be repaid
- (d) systems becoming insecure because attackers' tools and access improved, not because of flaws introduced when they were built

**5.** Why does Hoodlet say patching an agentic browser by updating the system prompt is a poor fix?

- (a) It is too expensive
- (b) It becomes whack-a-mole, because attackers eventually overcome each new prompt
- (c) System prompts can't be changed after release
- (d) It breaks the same-origin policy

**6.** As Hoodlet describes the rule his teams worked under for regulated diagnostic (IVD) devices, what happened if a vulnerability wasn't patched or mitigated within 60 days?

- (a) A fine was paid
- (b) Nothing; the deadline was advisory
- (c) A full recall was required
- (d) The device was moved to research-use-only status

**7.** What question did Hoodlet use to get development teams to take their vulnerabilities seriously?

- (a) Would you use a banking app that had the vulnerabilities your software has?
- (b) How much would a breach cost the company?
- (c) Have you read the OWASP Top 10?
- (d) Who wrote this code?
